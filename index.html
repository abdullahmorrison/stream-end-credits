5 · Add it to OBS

Six clicks. The fifth is the one people miss.

  1. Open your ending scene
  2. Sources → + → Browser
  3. Paste your link into URL
  4. Width 1920 · Height 1080
  5. Untick “Shutdown source when not visible” Required
  6. OK

Why step 5: the overlay collects names while it is loaded. Leave that box ticked and it only wakes up when you open the ending scene — too late to have seen anything, so the credits roll empty.

6 · Then nothing

  • Switch to your ending scene — the credits roll
  • Switch away — they stop
  • Nothing to type. No chat command, no button, no hotkey
  • Reloaded the source while that scene was already up? Switch away and back
