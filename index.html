Stream end credits

Thank everyone who showed up, as film credits, at the end of your stream. No download, no login, no account to connect.

1 · Your channel

The Twitch channel whose chat the overlay listens to.

2 · Options

Fine if you leave these alone.

3 · See it first

This is the real overlay, running on made-up names.

4 · Your link

Copy this, then follow the steps below.

5 · Add it to OBS

  1. Switch to the scene you use at the end of your stream.
  2. Under Sources, click + and choose Browser.
  3. Give it a name (End credits) and click OK.
  4. Paste your link into the URL box.
  5. Set Width to 1920 and Height to 1080.
  6. Untick “Shutdown source when not visible”, then click OK.

That last step is the one that matters. The overlay collects names the whole time it is loaded, so it has to keep running while you are on your other scenes. Leave that box ticked and it only starts when you switch to the ending scene — then rolls empty credits, because it was not there for any of the stream.

Nothing else to do, and nothing to type. Switch to that scene at the end of your stream and the credits roll. Switch away and they stop.

If you reload the source while the ending scene is already up, switch away and back to start it — OBS reports scene changes, so a page that has just loaded has no way to tell which scene is showing.

A section with nobody in it is left out, so a stream with no raids shows no “Raided by” heading.

Follows are not on this list. They are the one thing Twitch keeps out of chat, and reading them needs a login that expires every few hours — so the way you would find out it had gone stale is empty credits at the end of a stream. Everything above needs no login at all.
